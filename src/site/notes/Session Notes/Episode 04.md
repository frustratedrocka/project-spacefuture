# Episode 04 \- Puree

*First Wing's shore leave is interrupted by an attack from the mercenary company [[Database/Things/Blender's Edge\|Blender's Edge]]. This turns out to be a diversion meant to draw out [[Database/Places/Colonies/Hygiea\|Hygiea]]'s defenders while [[Database/People/Carlo Estashee\|Carlo Estashee]] and an accomplice launch an attack from within.*

> [!cite|bg-c-purple]+ Previously On *Zero Sum*...
> Let's start with the good news: the [[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]] is finally undergoing much-needed repairs. The industrial colony of [[Database/Places/Colonies/Hygiea\|Hygiea]] has agreed to conduct the (extensive) work in exchange for a favor: drive off the mercenary company [[Database/Things/Blender's Edge\|Blender's Edge]], who have been trying to break into the colony for reasons unknown.
> 
> That's odd behavior for would-be raiders. Usually, if you want something from a colony, all you have to do is broadcast your demands, get past the defenders, and point a big gun at something important until they give you what you ask for. Blender's Edge haven't done that, and Hygiea's leadership council say they've got no clue what they might be after.
> 
> Further complicating the matter is that Hygiea is not self sufficient, and currently relies on its relationship to [[Database/Factions/Apsis\|Apsis]] to feed its large refugee population. That means Apsis maintains a local presence and intelligence apparatus. [[Database/People/Gen Bashaba\|Cpt Bashaba]] has given very emphatic orders that you are to stay quiet, not attract attention, and avoid bringing the [[Database/Things/Brynhildr\|Brynhildr]] or other Apsis enforcement down on your heads at all costs.
> 
> So obviously the first order of business involved [[Player Characters/August Grier\|Auggie]] starting a bar fight and [[Player Characters/Menodora Thaliana\|Mena]] registering with the [[Database/Glossary/Fight Pits\|Fight Pits]] for a small local tournament. And, of course, Mena's first bout was well underway on the far side of the asteroid when everyone's datapads received the alert to scramble. 
> 
> Which means there's a good chance you're going into this down one pilot. How's everyone handling that circumstance as the elevator rushes you out of the colony's artificial gravity and towards the hangar in the central freight hub?

# Notes 

[[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]] undergoing repairs in [[Database/Places/Colonies/Hygiea\|Hygiea]], in exchange for driving off "[[Database/Things/Blender's Edge\|blender's edge]]" merc group.

Hygiea has no clue why blender's edge is trying to access their station.

[[Player Characters/Lane Gable\|Lane]] dropped off some supplies for an orphanage, [[Player Characters/Menodora Thaliana\|Mena]] was in a mech fight and [[Player Characters/August Grier\|Auggie]] was in a bar fight, so everyone was keeping a low profile. Blender's Edge attacked in the middle of Mena's match.

Vergen launches first, followed by Auggy and then Lane. Aided by 4 [[Database/Mobile Suits/TF-8 Hoplite\|hoplites]] from Hygeia.

Blender's edge seem to be mostly keeping pilots alive and only destroying the mechs.
Blender's edge has 2 [[Database/Mobile Suits/TF-8 Hoplite\|Hoplites]], 2 [[Database/Mobile Suits/MGU-20 Seeker\|Seekers]] and 1 [[Database/Mobile Suits/TF-8S Hoplite Striker\|Hoplite Striker]]. 

[[Database/People/Beaujolais Rigg\|Beaujolais Rigg]] - leader of blender's edge group (piloting striker). They state they have a "contract to fulfill" but will not say what that is.

[[Player Characters/Vergen Koni\|Verg]] and Auggy push up into debris field, and engage suppressive fire. 

Lane paints an enemy hoplite and Hygiea's militia open up on it, destroying it.

Enemy striker lands a hit on Auggy while he's distracted by the enemy hoplite, but Lane manages to destroy the hoplite as it accidentally collides with a piece of debris.

Enemy seeker is hidden in the debris field.

Group of friendly Hoplites take out the last enemy hoplite, unfortunately the pilot was unable to punch out fast enough.

Enemy seeker exits his stealth and attacks Auggy in the back with a masterful shot, damaging Auggy's shoulder. Rigg then also attacks Auggy and takes out his weapon.

Lane shoots at Rigg, destroying his arm and shoulder rocket pod.

hoplite group takes out last seeker and Rigg runs away.

We are radio'd that a hostile group of enemy suits are in the colony.

We pick up the escape pods of the enemy mercs, but they don't seem to know anything. We leave them with the people of Hygiea.

There is an unknown mobile suit and [[Database/Mobile Suits/MGU-23 Seeker CQC\|Seeker CQC]] inside the colony, near elevator 4.

We quickly make our way into the elevator shaft, and recognize the [[Database/Mobile Suits/KUD-20 Telomane\|Telomane]] from the mech fights.

We attempt to tell Auggy to run if he needs to, since he has taken damage, but he says he never runs away "never have, never will".

[[Database/People/Carlo Estashee\|Carlo Estashee]] is the pilot of the Telomane. Asks if we want to join them to "steal and wreck" things.

We initiate combat against the enemy suits, but they are very agile.

Cue the most anime fight scene that ever anime'd, and we eventually managed to squeak our way to a victory.

> [!example] HIGHLIGHT REEL
> - Auggie uses gravity screw of descending elevator + boosters to elbow drop Carlo, followed by the enemy Seeker CQC doing the same thing with a sword
> - Carlo pins the [[Database/Mobile Suits/MEU-1H Hyper Seeker CQC\|Hyper Seeker]] to the wall of the elevator shaft 1/4 of the way up with a katana through the upper chest, meaning Verg can't leave without exposing a hole and venting the shaft to space
> - Lane fries Carlo's servos and locks up his legs as elevator nears colony exit
> - Moments before elevator reaches bottom and Carlo tries to boost out anyway, Verg grapples the [[Database/Mobile Suits/KUD-20 Telomane\|Telomane]] from his pinned spot above and leaves him dangling in the shaft like a 20 meter piñata
