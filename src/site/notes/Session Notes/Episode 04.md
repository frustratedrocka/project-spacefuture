# Episode 04 \- 

*First Wing's shore leave is interrupted by an attack from the mercenary company [[Database/Things/Blender's Edge\|Blender's Edge]].*

!/r!

#

> [!cite|bg-c-purple]+ Previously On *Zero Sum*...
> Let's start with the good news: the [[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]] is finally undergoing much-needed repairs. The industrial colony of [[Database/Places/Colonies/Hygiea\|Hygiea]] has agreed to conduct the (extensive) work in exchange for a favor: drive off the mercenary company [[Database/Things/Blender's Edge\|Blender's Edge]], who have been trying to break into the colony for reasons unknown.
> 
> That's odd behavior for would-be raiders. Usually, if you want something from a colony, all you have to do is broadcast your demands, get past the defenders, and point a big gun at something important until they give you what you ask for. Blender's Edge haven't done that, and Hygiea's leadership council say they've got no clue what they might be after.
> 
> Further complicating the matter is that Hygiea is not self sufficient, and currently relies on its relationship to [[Database/Factions/Apsis\|Apsis]] to feed its large refugee population. That means Apsis maintains a local presence and intelligence apparatus. [[Database/People/Gen Bashaba\|Cpt Bashaba]] has given very emphatic orders that you are to stay quiet, not attract attention, and avoid bringing the [[Database/Things/Brynhildr\|Brynhildr]] or other Apsis enforcement down on your heads at all costs.
> 
> So obviously the first order of business involved [[Player Characters/August Grier\|Auggie]] starting a bar fight and [[Player Characters/Menodora Thaliana\|Mena]] registering with the [[Database/Glossary/Fight Pits\|Fight Pits]] for a small local tournament. And, of course, Mena's first bout was well underway on the far side of the asteroid when everyone's datapads received the alert to scramble. 
> 
>Which means there's a good chance you're going into this down one pilot. How's everyone handling that circumstance as the elevator rushes you out of the colony's artificial gravity and towards the hangar in the central freight hub?
