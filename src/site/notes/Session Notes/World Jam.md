# World Jam \- A Century Emerges
*An impromptu worldbuilding session before character creation*

# Notes


## Requirements
- Backstory: There was an enormous crisis in the past, and whatever organization you're resisting was formed as a massive overcorrection to that problem
- Present day: That organization has caused the exact crisis it was supposed to prevent. Zero ambiguity about whether they are actually the bad guys or whether war against them is justified; they are and it is.. Not interested in constantly interrogating the campaign's premise.
