# Session 01 \- And So It Begins

*[[Database/People/Vantrin Almeyer\|Vantrin Almeyer]] launches a surprise attack on the [[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]], decapitating its command team in a single strike. Second wing is forced to step up in a desperate scramble to save the ship.*

# Notes


Year - 0092

The Hivemind began 20 years ago, and made everyone curious as to how it would affect humanity.
14 years ago, the moon hit the Earth
11 years ago The War ended
8 months ago, Apsis cut off the food to three rebel colonies.

The Fishbone - Small, tough to get rid of, and once it gets stuck in, impossible to get rid of
We are Second Wing, a backup to First Wing. We have already had two successful raids, and are currently trying to outrun the Apsis ship the Brynhildr.

Then - The Bridge Explodes

Lane Gable was taking a bath

August was smoking a cigarette in some crawlspace

Vergen was window-watching admiring the view of space. Being on the even side of the ship, he saw the massive flash of light moments before the bridge was hit.

Menodora had her legs hooked up somewhere messing with the code of her Gundam to see if she could improve it

As the ship rocks, our XO Artel Ward gets over the tannoy as the Captain is presumably dead

“ [List of names, get from Ben] Report to CIC immediately, First and Second Wing, to hangers”

Vergen and Menodora meet up, Vergen going “Are you ready to go?!”

Menodora “I have been fucking ready”

Lane is struggling to beat to the hanger while composing himself from the bath.

Auggie has appeared out of a crawlspace and is silently running alongside us.

Vergen “I saw this blast, it was a giant fucking laser.”

Menodora “It rocked our shit.”

Vergen “Whatever it is, it’s hammering us hard!”

Vergen always has his flight suit on, with a multicolor trenchcoat over it currently flapping in the breeze.

All of August’s clothes are oversized and ill-fitting.

Menodora had the jumpsuit on with the arms tied around the waist, wrestling into it as they make their way down.

First Obstacle

An emergency bulkhead is sealed in front of us so we can’t get through our main route.

Menodora goes If you can build it, I can break it. Pointing out she grew up on ships and has a bunch of self-taught engineering and coding skills. While the roll is a success, there is a complication and that complication is a collapsed wall and debris in the hallway.

Delbrow Enner is pinned under debris in front of us. Vergen, recognizing Del, goes to lift the debris off and help. He rolls a success with style. Verden uses his knowledge of leverage to effortlessly move the debris off and pull Del out.

(Daeon uses the boost to remind Del Don’t just think of us as backups, we’re competent too)

Del runs off to go to CiC, we are going through the bulkhead but there is still a partially-collapsed debris field.

Lane attempts to Notice-Cleverly a safe path to go through. There is a path we will have to go through carefully to avoid our suits being punctured and tearing, but we are making our way through the debris field.

Menodora gets a Fate point to have her suit snagged on some of the debris and tear a little at the thigh halfway between the hip and the knee. It is not a danger in the current atmosphere but is a massive risk.

We resume making our way down the corridor. The ship continues to shake around us while the XO is barking orders over the tannoy.

Then, eighty feet ahead of us, the corridor fills with heat as a beam saber pierces the hull.

“HOLY SHIT!” “FUCK!

Auggie goes to protect us from the beam, believing his life is expendable. (Spends a fate point). The leads to us being checked down a corridor to protect us though August gets hit by an explosion and slammed into something, taking a massive blow and getting an injury.

The beam probably cored a mech, considering now the section we are in is now venting atmosphere. Menodora spends a fate point to spot that there is clean room for handling repairing more sensitive components of mecha, and that it has a sealable atmosphere and a backway to get to the hanger.

4 dead Akonis floating in space. One of First Wing was killed and their Gundam coring is what blew the hole. Vergen gives Menodora a roll of tape to take a second to patch up. It succeeds but it’s not a strong patch.

Lane is attempting to Interface Quickly with a computer to get us to the hanger.

Vergen is strapping on his helmet and launching himself to get to his mobile suit as fast as possible, getting to the airlock before the hangers to get suits on and cycle through.

Another member of First Wing goes down, and we can feel the ship shake from the blast even if we’re not sure how many Akonis there still are.

We get our suits on and are rushing through the airlock to get to our mecha.

We are about to do the fastest pre-flight checks possible, but what do we refuse to not skip?

Menodora refuses to fly without checking her chaff launcher
August checks his weapons and once he gets the OK he goes
Lane checks his weapons and makes sure he can boost+use thrusters
Vergen doesn’t skip thrusters or weapons but is currently skipping sensors

Auggie’s superstition is he never puts his helmets on before seating, and also smacks a shattered console before going

Menodora kisses two fingers of right hand and taps the console before going

Lane does a little meditation to get in pre-flight mode

Hoplite Custom gets on the launcher first, and goes “August Grier - Cossack - Launching” before being shot to space

Lane - Elegant Sky - launches next

Vergen - Hyper Seeker - launches

Menodora - Kerbstomp - launches

3 normal Akonis and one command type remain, and only the Gundam Makairos is missing one arm and both legs. Makairos attempts one desperate last move by spinning and launching itself, and while it kills a mech it explodes. The escape pod is damaged so we don’t know if they survived even though they did have a successful separation.

“This is Lieutenant Commander Vantrin Allmeyer Commander of the Brynhildr, you have the chance to surrender. Stand down and you will be spared.”

We all tell him in our own ways that we refuse to surrender.

Menodora is going to try and damage one of the grunt mechs
August is going to shoot at the one Menodora is engaging with
Verg is speeding towards the Commander in attempt to do a flurry of strikes
Lane is going to attempt to take on the Commander via sniping, taking advantage of Vergen attacking and hopefully distracting him


Vantrin gets an unnamed boost trying to handle Lane and Verg
Menodora ties with the Akoni and it is tied up
Because it is tied up, when August goes to shoot with a massive advantage. He shoots it in the back three times and has Meno kick it away before it explodes in her face.

The other Akoni attempts to shoot a lot of suppressive fire into Vergen’s back. Vergen manages to dodge and is completely fine.

Vantrin attempts to back away but Vergen is making sure to stay on him. Vergen gets a catastrophic failure dealing with Vantrin, who gives Vergen 2 harm slashing at his mobile suit attempting to barrel past him

Lane goes to shoot Vantrin, and even with Underdogs Can Win being invoked, doesn’t succeed the roll. Works to make sure he lands out of Vantrin’s view.

Menodora is going for the other Akoni mech and engages her drill in an attempt to core it.

Lane is planning to surprise Vartrin by sneaking up on him closing in to melee.
Vergen is attempting to slash Vantrin

August is planning to help Menodora with attacking the Arkoni

Now we enter the fire phase.

Vantrin attempts to stab August in the back. August attempts to counter with Front Towards Enemy

Vergen attempts to fight Vantrin and is nearly overpowering him. While Vantrin attempts to use Ice in his veins but Vergen counters and harms Vantrin.

August goes to ram the remaining grunt with his body and stuns the grunt with a free invoke. August knees the cockpit to stun the pilot.

Menadora takes her drill and cores the mech, and her mech is built to withstand being at ground zero for that as she has done it so many times in brawls.

Lane attempts to take out Vantrin, and goes to damage him as much as possible. His armor is blown through and his suit is dented.

Vantrin concedes at the top of the round. He loses, and burns a full retreat back to the Brynhildr.

The fight is over, all grunt Akonis are dead, the fight is over but while that is happening the Fishbone is crippled and limping.

Menodora calls Vantrin and goes, “Yeah we can see our ship and so can you, you fuck off and let us fuck off and we’ll leave you alone. Or you’ll wish we’d cored your mech and kill you in the explosion.”


Now we’re back to the Fishbone

We manage to find Anatole Darza (Makairos’ pilot) in the escape pod and bring him back, but he’s catatonic as he’s rushed to medbay.

As the engines of the ship shudder back to life, our XO’s voice comes over the loudspeakers and requests Second Wing to CiC while all other hands are sent to repair stations.

Helmets stay on due to the damage as we make our way to CiC. XO Ward, alongside Jen Bashaba, a competent up and coming young officer, now in Captain’s stripes. The rest of the bridge crew is similar, as XO Ward has needed to brevet an entirely new command staff as most of the old staff was killed when the bridge went up.

Now-Captain Bashaba speaks. “There’s nothing I can say, to make what happened today not a disaster. We were hit out of nowhere, and we were hit hard. We are alive because all of us who are left worked together, trusted each other, picked up the slack supported each other.” She looks at all of us in turn.

“New Captain 

“New helmsman, Hao Nebreka.”

“New Chief of Engineering, Gracie-Ella Tour.”

The Captain continues, “We will mourn, and we will endure, and we will win, and we will do it together. I’m counting on you all, dismissed.”

Ward speaks up as we attempt to leave the room. “Second Wing, excuse me, hang back please.”

Lane asks for permission to speak after the room is cleared, leaving us with Ward and Bashaba. Lance asks, “How the hell did they catch us off guard?”

Ward “That is the exact question that should be asked. No weapons that needed to held that still to be fired would need to be ship-mounted, not suit mounted.”

Above-Board, the blackbox tech that makes mobile suits make sense in Gundam is the MV Field. It fucks up targeting hence why suits are needed to get in close and strike craft are dominant.

Cloaking does not exist, at least nowhere near that good.

Which means there is only one explanation Ward can give us. “We have a traitor.”
