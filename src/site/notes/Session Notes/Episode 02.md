# Episode 02 \- The Saboteur

 

*As the Fishbone limps towards drydock and the crew mourn the fallen, newly-promoted First Wing identifies [[Database/People/Delbrau Enger\|Delbrau Enger]] as the traitor and struggles over what to do about him.*


> [!note|bg-c-purple]- Housekeeping
> - Retroactive compel: August's trouble pulled him into danger and caused him harm; +1 Fate point
> - PC Free Aspect slot now reserved for a **CONNECTION** with something external to yourself

> [!cite|bg-c-purple]+ Previously On *Zero Sum*...
> 
> Your ship has just been brutalized. The Apsis cruiser Brynhildr's mobile suit team, led by Lt. Commander [[Database/People/Vantrin Almeyer\|Vantrin Almeyer]], pulled off an impossible attack that obliterated the [[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]]'s bridge and command staff, and subsequently all but wiped out First Wing. [[Database/People/Anatol Garza\|Anatol Garza]], living legend of [[Database/History/The Ground War\|The Ground War]] and one of the [[Database/Factions/Rebels\|Rebels]]' top aces, is in a coma; his [[Database/Mobile Suits/EW-14 Gundam Makhairos\|Gundam Makhairos]] is largely space debris. You managed to make it to the hangar and fight off the last of the attackers before your ship could be destroyed completely, but the damage is done. 
> 
> And the limitations of this world's technology mean that it could only have been done with help from someone currently aboard the ship. 
> 
> You, being technically outside the normal shipboard chain of command, have been asked to help conduct the investigation. 
> 
> But, more immediately: due to the gaping hole blown in the hull by an exploding mobile suit, a large portion of the ship's port compartments are currently off-limits to keep any more oxygen from venting. This section includes your quarters. Of the things that got left there when the attack hit, what do you feel worst about not having access to right now? 

 



# Notes 
Previously on Gundam, the ship was Brutalized. [[Database/People/Anatol Garza\|Anatol Garza]] is now in a coma after what the ship has faced.

Now the fires have been put out, the new command staff is starting to settle into place, and the gaping hole in the hull is currently cordoned off. We are now not even being told where we’re going due to the threat of a mole on-board.

Due to the hole, even our quarters are off limits. [[Player Characters/Menodora Thaliana\|Menodora]] is missing her painkillers, and [[Player Characters/August Grier\|August]] cannot get to his small hoard in his quarters. [[Player Characters/Lane Gable\|Lane]] is missing his cigars and [[Player Characters/Vergen Koni\|Vergen]] is missing his headphones as that means he can’t use his music player.

Now there is legwork to be done as the only remaining people outside the command structure of the ship, the investigation falls to us by default.

August says we need to investigate anyone who had access to the sensor logs, which is the bridge or the CiC. The ships internet is extremely limited, think airplane wi-fi, and we need to talk to our new Comms officer, [[Database/People/Delbrau Enger\|Delbrau Enger]]. Considering his leg is still in a cast but we did save his life, he will probably be amenable to talking to us.

Del is currently eating and looking glum, and he is known to never shut up about his family and especially his wife. Or at least, he was talking a lot about her, until we docked where she was and he was, rumor has it, separated in some fashion from her.

Vergen attempts to connect with him over his new promotion. Luckily Del is having the same suspicions, and offers to help about the logs however he can. Everyone is still in shell-shock and we hear that we need to talk to [[Database/People/Mashola Thorne\|Mashola Thorne]].

Vergen is feeling paranoid and is looking around. Mostly everyone is on-edge, so it’s hard to tell who is suspicious. Vergen is getting the paranoid feeling that Del is not quite being as forthcoming and might be the mole.

Meanwhile, Menadora is planning to an EVA to check for any information on the bridge. First, Menadora needs a quick chat with the lead hanger-tech to get the hatch open. That is [[Database/People/Micah Brann\|Micah Brann]], and he is someone Menadora has worked a lot with.

Menadora offers to do a favor while out there, and Micah asks to update on any minor repairs if she spots them on the way out. Menadora’s suit is patched up, and as they make their way to the bridge, and with all the damage we’re having difficulty figuring out where things are on the bridge.

Menadora decides to also try and find the bridge’s black box, and uses her engineering skills to figure out where it would be most sensible to put it and manages to find the blackbox.

Lane is working to find the computer, and manages to plug into some wires and go through the code and files. He does find evidence of tampering, including a worm in the sensor systems. The worm is keyed to recognize [[Database/Factions/Apsis\|Apsis]] suits as microasteroids instead of mobile suits. This was probably installed no more than three days before we were attacked, but that is all Lane can determine before it starts to try and infect the [[Database/Glossary/Datapad\|datapad]] he is using.

Lane is given the choice to try and counter it, which might fry the datapad, or unplug. Lane manages to fight the worm and disable it, saving the datapad. He also learns the worm was installed 2 days before the attack, and it was not uploaded physically on the bridge. Lane wants to get the logs from here on the bridge to compare the logs that we are given by Mash.

Menadora is scrubbing through the blackbox data to see if there is any sign of a struggle, and as far as the recording of the bridge is concerned they were all fine one moment, one moment of horrified screaming as they notice the light, a loud whoosh, then the silence of space.

So because the mole was not on the bridge as far as we are aware, we know they are probably still alive among the crew. Lane decides to try and check who is new on the crew, and we decide it is time to head back into the ship.

As Vergen finishes his meal, he overhears multiple people gossiping, to the tune of “Oh crap they put Neckbreaker on the stick we’re all going to die.” That is a reference to our newest pilot, [[Database/People/Howe Nebreka\|Howe Nebreka]], who is *enthusiastic* with piloting.

As Vergan and Del discuss Del’s broken leg, Vergen gets the message about the worm we found in the bridge. Then Vergen goes “When we found you in the hall, were you planning to go to the bridge or somewhere else?”

Del “I was off-shift, and it all happened so fast. One minute I’m there, the next minute, the hallway explodes on me.”

Vergen spends his fate point due to his paranoia, and realizes something is not adding up. There is something he is not telling. Del tries to brush it off to go get the logs and Vergen is now very paranoid.

Vergen and Del go the current CiC and Neshaba is still adjusting to being the new acting Captain. With all the chaos of who has been killed we are adjusting to our own new ranks. Del is trying to avoid giving off any signals despite the spectacularly bad month he is having, though Vergen is still suspicious and Menadora is feeling off too.

(`REDACTED` establishes by spending a Fate Point to send an emotional Rebus to Vergen about what they discovered on the bridge. The caveats with this skill is it is just emotions and impressions, and that you have to be within a reasonable distance of the other person.)

Vergen is looking over Del’s shoulder and is seeing he is navigating to two days ago to pull the comms logs. There don’t appear to have anything out of the ordinary, and Vergen asks Del to pull the logs for a week back.

Vergen has somewhat enhanced perception, and while Del is hitting the back button a lot Vergen is seeing holes in the comms data. Slowing down Del, Vergen sees there are gaps of two hours over a few days. The fact that there are gaps in the data at all is quite suspicious, and Vergen asks Del to start pulling names and copying the logs for him.

Del is stressed as fuck, and is trying to play it off that is stressed because there are gaps. But it’s not quite convincing to Vergen. Vergen summons the rest of Second Wing, who have been transiting through the airlock. Vergen also sends a message to Auggie telling him to watch Del and the door.

The rest of Second Wing gets to the CiC, and Auggie starts smoking and leaning against the door. Normally that is not allowed but Bashaba knows something is about to go down so she’s letting it slide.

Vergen flicks the information from his datapad to a larger screen and is explaining it while Del is trying to point out all the missing points. Menadora points out on the bridge they learned the mole was not there, and Lane explains how the worm made apsis appear to be micrometeors. Lane dug into it more after disabling the worm, and explained when it was uploaded and that it was not on the bridge.

Which means this person was either in CiC or on the bridge. And the two hour gaps stop the day before the attack. Lane thinks that we need to check with Mashala Thorne on the data. Also now that Verg is suspicious of Del, Lane is getting suspicious. And we start to turn the screws on Del.

Auggie invokes “Front Towards Enemy” as he goes to intimidate Del, and Del finally breaks under pressure. Del starts searching our faces, and crumples as he notices the look on Vergen’s face. He is a mixture of anguish and relief.

“Allmeyer has my family.” He explains how much of his family Apsis has been holding hostage, with proof of life. Nebreka launches himself out of the chair in Zero-G to try and beat the shit out of Del. Vergen intercepts Nebreka’s punches to stop him, and goes “Helmsman Nebreka, we are a family here. If you want to be more of a problem, you can fight me-”

“You wanna try that mate?!” -Nebreka

Bashaba makes everyone stand down, though everyone is still on edge and Del is just relieved.

Lane goes “I understand your family is in danger, but now that you’ve done this, how can you guarantee their safety?”

Del starts to confess before pulling out his own datapad and putting the message fromm Allmeyer up on the screen.

"Your failure to exfiltrate is neither my fault nor my problem. However, the survival of the ship is neither your fault nor your problem. So the arrangement stands: your spouse will be released as soon as doing so no longer risks your cover, and you are still owed an extraction. When you reach your destination, wherever it is, locate the Apsis liason and request a meeting with the Deputy Actuarial Coordinator. We'll take it from there." 

Del “My family is still alive according to the handshake, this is legit.”

Lane “What prevents them from killing you when you are extracted?”

Del “Nothing but Allmeyer’s word, which so far he’s stood by.”

Lane “This could be an opportunity, we could use Del as a double agent if they think he is uncompromised.”

We decide to make a plan to use Del as bait for Allmeyer and the Brynhildr. Del accepts he’s pretty much a dead man walking no matter what. August is ordered to escort Del to the brig.

Lane is going to have a rough time sticking to his guns on not executing Delbrau because everyone wants his head.

We are dealing with the fact this rebellion is unstable and the torn feelings on Del are not going to fix this.

Menodora and Vergen get into a heated argument, and it escalates too much due to both being [[Database/History/The Hive Mind\|Hive]] they get into a feedback loop, which gives them both a migraine.

Lane tries to calm the situation down, and Menodora needs to excuse herself to leave from how bad her migraine is.

Auggie is leading Del to the brig and explains that he’s lucky other people are choosing to be kinder to him, as Auggie would have executed him.

Menodora removes herself to the Medbay as she still can’t go to her quarters, and needs a cot and painkillers to handle the migraine. [[Database/People/Parlica Replize\|Parlica Replize]] is the current duty nurse on the roster, who gives Menodora a painkiller and a spot to lie down.

Verg gets a message from [[Database/People/Graciela Tor\|Tor]] the new chief engineer going “What the hell was that?!” as people are dismissed from the bridge.

Bashaba lets the stoneface drop a bit once it is just her and Verg in the CiC. Bashaba thanks him for doing good work despite how painful it was. As he leaves, he opens his messages to find one from Tor that outs her as Hive. (She also runs the ships semi-underground poker ring.)

Lane gets an EVA suit to get his cigars from his currently atmosphere-vented room.

Graciela Tor starts to interrogate Vergen on explaining what the fuck just happened in the hivemind. He explains what happened with Del, and it takes her a moment to process that before explaining it should stay secret until the captain handles it.
