# Episode 03 \- Shore Leave

*The [[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]] makes port at [[Database/Places/Colonies/Hygiea\|Hygiea]], with an arrangement in place in exchange for repairs and nothing pressing in the meantime. Enjoy shore leave!*

> It's been a little over a week since [[Database/People/Delbrau Enger\|Delbrau Enger]] confessed to the sabotage of the [[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]]'s sensor systems. That act allowed [[Database/People/Vantrin Almeyer\|Vantrin Almeyer]] and the [[Database/Things/Brynhildr\|Brynhildr]]'s away team to strike the bridge, wipe out its command staff, and slaughter the aces of the original First Wing. 
> 
> You are what's left. And, under the circumstances, you've been doing an admirable job. 
> 
> As the wounded ship continues its slouch towards drydock at a still-need-to-know colony, tensions are running high. The word about Del is out, and much of the crew is baying for his blood. There's especially strong discontent that, in the words of the loudest objectors, a week's worth of food that could have gone somewhere deserving is being wasted to preserve a traitor.
> 
> [[Player Characters/Menodora Thaliana\|Mena]], you're dealing with an additional problem. Your heated argument with [[Player Characters/Vergen Koni\|Verg]] got out of hand enough that every other hiver on the ship felt it. They don't know it's you, but they know there's another of their own aboard that they apparently haven't identified. So far, it doesn't seem like anyone's prying, but it's hard not to keep the concern in mind. What are you most worried about? 
> 
> [[Player Characters/Vergen Koni\|Verg]], you've been dealing with the aftermath of the ship's new chief engineer, [[Database/People/Graciela Tor\|Graciela Tor]], revealing herself to you as another member of the hive. Does your status as a Mindful Eye create any obligations towards her now that you know?
> 
> [[Player Characters/August Grier\|Auggie]], you've made it clear that if it had been your call, the Del conversation would've been over a week ago. Which may have something to do with why you and several others are being kept busy with make-work as far from the brig as possible. Are you planning to do anything about it, or will you respect the barely-reforged chain of command?
> 
> [[Player Characters/Lane Gable\|Lane]], you've said you believe more problems can be solved without violence than people generally assume. The past week has been one of the rougher tests of that assumption you've dealt with recently. What's your take on the whole mess?

# Notes 

It has been a week since the betrayal, we are limping to drydock but tensions are high and people want [[Database/People/Delbrau Enger\|Delbrau]]’s blood. People are especially pissed that Del is getting a week of food so far that could have gone to others.

“We do not decide who deserves to eat that is the point.” -[[Database/People/Gen Bashaba\|Cpt Bashaba]]

[[Player Characters/Menodora Thaliana\|Menodora]]-
Every other [[Database/History/The Hive Mind\|Hiver]] now knows that there is another on the ship they don’t know about and they’re trying to find out who, Menadora is trying to keep it under wraps because it’s her personal status and she is focused on survival, explicitly not a member of [[Database/Factions/Mindful Eyes\|Mindful Eyes]] as she uses them sometimes to move between planets but does not consider herself a part of the org.

[[Player Characters/Vergen Koni\|Vergen]]-
Has been dealing with [[Database/People/Graciela Tor\|Graciela Tor]]now also revealed as a Hive, he is trying to not make a big deal about it but it is giving him pause as a member of [[Database/Factions/Mindful Eyes\|Mindful Eyes]] as he wants to keep a cautious eye on things to prevent making the situation worse.

[[Player Characters/Lane Gable\|Lane]]-
Believes more things can be solved without violence than people assume, something that has been tested the past week. Lane understands not wanting his family threatened but people died, however Lane cannot help having empathy for Delbrau.

As the ship continues the slog through the void Lane is called to the CiC to meet with the Captain and XO [[Database/People/Artel Ward\|Ward]]. Ward is the first to speak.

“I heard you had an idea to use Del to bait out the [[Database/Things/Brynhildr\|Brynhildr]].”

Lane “I thought it would be useful.”

Ward “Whatever it is will need to wait for repairs to be complete, but we’re curious what you have in mind.”

Lane “As Del stated in the past he was supposed to meet with the Brynhildr to exchange for his family. As we know where the Brynhildr will be from that, we can lay a trap and jump at the chance for it.”

Ward “Whenever we get where we’re going, he was supposed to speak to the local [[Database/Factions/Apsis\|Apsis]] liaison, give them a code phrase and be handled from there. So the Brynhildr would need to make a beeline to that colony to make the exchange.”

However to lay a trap on their route we would need to know where they’re coming from, and if the meeting is a place like a space station or putting our backs to a colony that’s a very bad idea.

(Cue discussion on potential world-building with orbital mechanics from resident former rocketry-student Menadora’s Player)

Captain Bashaba decides to attempt to brevet Lane up to First Lieutenant/Wing Commander, but he is worried about the fact that the previous times he has been in charge people have died. She’ll keep it in mind but for now they don’t have a lot of options.

Lane leaves to go help handle repairs.

The next day the announcement finally goes out over the ship’s speakers. “Attention crew, we are within comms distance of our destination we are docking at the colony of [[Database/Places/Colonies/Hygiea\|Hygiea]]. We’ll be in drydock outside proper range of the colony, it is sympathetic to the [[Database/Factions/Rebels\|Rebels]] but it is still under Apsis control.

First Wing you’ll be briefed separately remain ready. Essential personnel stay aboard for repairs, everyone else gets shore leave. Keep an extremely low profile, if Apsis finds out we’re here before we’re repaired things are going to go very wrong very quickly.”

Vergen has never been to Hygiea, Menadora probably stopped in once on the run, Lane has stopped in over his time pirating.

Hygiea is a relatively small colony focused on heavy industry, they do a lot of subcontracting and boring but essential mechanical components. Things like inertial dampeners, gyroscopes, motor parts.

Mena’s familiarity with Hygiea is that it is a relatively known spot on the fight pit circuit. Menadora (Player chose to roll to see how that went) fought well but it was a hard won victory and she won by the skin of her teeth, needing to pull some of her stunts with her mech to win.

We are back in the CiC room, outside of Lane it is our first time back in since the [[Session Notes/Episode 02\|Delbrau Incident]].

Command cut a deal with Hygiea, they will shelter us and keep things quiet as we get patched up as fast as we can, but in exchange we need to help them with a bandit problem.

Here’s what we know-

Hygiea has been dealing with a mercenary company called [[Blender’s Edge\|Blender’s Edge]]
(We learn that Lane avoided attacking and pillaging colonies in his days as a [[Database/Factions/Armada Ejecta\|pirate]], as it is something even [[Database/People/The Pirate King\|the Pirate King]] frowns on.)
(August’s Player arrives at this moment, quick detour to handle character things)
[[Player Characters/August Grier\|August]] also fought against Blender’s Edge and even though it has been years if they recognize his mech they are going to have a problem

Ward saw serious action during and after [[Database/History/The Ground War\|the Ground War]] so he knows what August dealt with dealing with the mercenary company

What Hygiea is finding weird is normally raiders point large guns at sensitive systems and do Gunboat Diplomacy.

What is strange is they are not doing that, they are very determined to get into the colony. So either they want the colony intact or it is something inside the colony they actually want, and whatever it is Hygiea isn’t telling us and Blender’s Edge doesn’t want to risk it.

We are going to be shuttled down to the [[Database/Glossary/Colony\|Colony]] with our mobile suits, our mobile suits will be kept in a central hub ready to deploy the moment the mercenaries show up.

Our primary objective is to stop the raids and attempt to keep them from happening again.

Hygea is pretty willing to allow us broad leeway on how we handle things as long as they are handled.

At least we will get to enjoy shore leave in gravity, to which Menadora complains her joints are going to hurt and requests permission to bring extra painkillers with her.

We are packed into a transport with our mecha and are met by the usual team of mobile suit longshoreman who handle bringing our suits to the staging area.

We are now in “Hurry up and wait mode”

Menadora decides to check in on contacts to see what’s new in mech components and if they have things to help improve her mech

Lane wants to explore the station a bit and maybe get a drink, see the sights. Lane also wants to stop by orphanages and pet shops. Lane always fought as a pirate because he wanted to get resources for the downtrodden in his stations, and he wants to see how people are here.

August wants to go to a Red Light district, not for services but because cramped spaces and lots of people help him feel better.

Vergen has been keeping to himself even during the debrief. He’s been in his own head and hearing about potential attacks he doesn’t want to peel himself away too far as he’s feeling paranoid and in a rough spot. To prioritize speed of response Vergen is going to stay by the elevators in the industrial area.

Lane is delivering some extra supplies to orphans during his downtime, at the Al-Saachez Home for Orphaned and Abandoned Children. Lane is sharing supplies from his own stash to help is somewhat of a production as trying to make sure it gets to the right area is hard as people are still traumatized from the Ground War and might still try to snatch it.

Lane has to roll to see if he can bring the food to the orphanage unbothered. He manages to roll so well he gets a car full of food to the orphanage quietly. Considering his past he has connections to the orphanages so he contacts someone in charge to deliver the food. Lane’s delivery is a welcome surprise to Fibria Wobik who runs the orphanage.

Lane lets her know why he’s here and asks if she knows why the mercenaries are so focused on the station, and she’s not sure but assumes it’s some kind of tech that they want.

Cut to August 

Most colonies are cramped these days by any reasonable standards due to most colonies at least tripling what their intended capacity was due to the refugee crisis from the war. Hygiea is not that large to begin with but ended up with a greater-than-average population, and while some access streets are large enough to allow construction mobile-suits most if the colony is stacked layers with artificial light. The most densely packed areas of refugee housing are known as The Cubes and look like a somewhat more functional Kowloon Walled City. They were meant to be temporary but they have now turned to more permanent solutions.

August decides to go to Fleming's, a local jazz club, some form of dingy smoky loud sublevel bar for people with friends in low places. The music is chaotic and not that melodic as the band isn’t great but it is a vibe all its own and the type August was looking for.

August is enjoying people-watching and is observing enough chatter+social interaction that he overhears people are betting on an upcoming pitfight. A decent sized tournament is coming up soon. August listens in to see about getting information for Menadora. The signup won’t be open much longer but apparently one or two new suit designs are being debuted and people are curious on the designs.

August decides to send Menadora a photo of the signup list, but gets kicked out for shoving someone away to get the photo that leads to a bar altercation.

Menadora recognizes the person she barely beat last time is a local and they are entered on the list. There’s no big names but there’s a couple she’s heard of. There’s one in particular she hasn’t fought but has a reputation as [[Database/People/Carlo Estashee\|a dirty fighter and an asshole]]. The dirty fighter also has one of the new mecha.

The prize is a new part for the mecha, something juicy and hard to come by.

Menadora is compelled by “No Such Thing As A ‘Hard No’” to be insubordinate and attend the mecha battle.

Vergen hears about the new mecha the “[[Database/Mobile Suits/KUD-20 Telomane\|Telomane]]” and decides to investigate this new mech and see what is new in it. The pit fights are recorded via camera feed due to the fights being in space and there is no such thing as “live-streaming” between planetary bodies. It will be broadcast but not live outside the colony.

He’s staying in his head and not talking to anyone as he is trying to stay mentally prepared for the fight and mulling over the “[[Session Notes/Scenarios/Rats In The Cellar\|Rats in the Cellar]]” incident.

We now cut back to the aftermath of the barfight that August was in, and while he was knocked on his ass it’s nothing he can’t walk off. However, because the attack succeeded with style, Auggie gets his ass beat. We now have an 8-clock for [[Database/Factions/Apsis\|Apsis]] Awareness.

We are now cutting the camera to the Enemy.

Meanwhile on the [[Database/Things/Brynhildr\|Brynhildr]] we are cutting to [[Database/People/Vantrin Almeyer\|a young man]] with white hair and a near-perpetual scowl as he looks at a Comm Screen.

“Yes… yes.. No, eight [[Database/Mobile Suits/SE-832 Akoni\|Akonis]] completely destroyed, another five damaged but recoverable, no 10 fatalities, 2 recovered, one still in critical condition. My recommendation, stay the course, I gave instructions and I expect those instructions to be obeyed. Because this doesn’t work otherwise that’s why, you know this. Right, continue to wait for the asset to come in from the cold then. Understood, over and out.” As soon as the call cuts out he rubs the bridge of his nose in frustration. “Idiots. The whole point of working with hostages is that the deal has to *work*.” He calls out “Any reports in yet? No? Keep me informed then.”

Now back to the [[Database/Things/Fishbone\|Fishbone]] crew.

We cut forward a few days. Menadora has been trying to keep a low profile, and so she signed up under a fake name and didn’t put her mech name. It’s kind of a known thing in the colonies that as long as the fighting doesn’t damage the colony it can help bring in some revenue.

Lane is going to watch the mech fight via a local broadcast, in the local equivalent of a sports bar. August and Vergen decide to watch it through the datapad as he has been “on-duty” wherever for the days since we docked. Auggie comes up with a split lip and a black eye.
3 rounds eight fighters total, first round is nothing spectacular [[Local Champion\|Local Champion]] wins first. Second round, for those watching, has [[Database/Mobile Suits/Kerbstomp\|Kerbstomp]].

Vergen was having a drink watching and spits his drink seeing Kerbstomp. August “you were watching the mecha and didn’t realize they’d move Kerbstomp?”

Kerbstomp is fighting a [[Database/Mobile Suits/TF-8 Hoplite\|Hoplite]] with an axe with the heat blade off and a missile launcher with the explosives removed. Kerbstomp attempts to kick the missile launchers off the mech and misses. The next round Kerbstomp heats up the end of the rebar to attract the heat-seeking missiles. Kerbstomp then attempts to force the other pilot out of the boundaries, though that fails.

The other pilot attempts to get behind Kerbstomp to use its momentum against Menadora. Kerbstomp uses its boost to prevent that from happening.

All the datapads go off at once “Scramble, Blender’s Edge incoming”

Menadora calls a pause with the other mech to go fight to save the colony, telling the [[Promoter\|organizers]] “Look the fights been even, we’ll come back and finish when we’re done, we need to stop the colony from being blown up.”

“Feeds been cut, save our asses”

Off we rocket to save the colony, end of session
