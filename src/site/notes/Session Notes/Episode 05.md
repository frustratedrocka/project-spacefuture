> The defense of Hygiea was a near-complete success.

> [[Database/Factions/Blender's Edge\|Blender's Edge]] and its leader, [[Database/People/Beaujolais Rigg\|Beaujolais Rigg]], were successfully driven off, with no casualties among your friendlies, and two ejected mercenaries taken prisoner. > 
> However. Just because the defense was successful doesn't mean it was unproblematic. Your flight was notably down one member, since [[Player Characters/Menodora Thaliana\|Menodora Thaliana]] was too far on the wrong side of the asteroid to intervene in either stage of the fight. After their first failed raid or two, they found out about the Tournament, contacted Carlo, and offered him a significant percentage and a new prototype MS if he'd subcontract and launch an attack from the inside under cover of tournament registration.
