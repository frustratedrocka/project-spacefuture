# Lines
- Violence against children (targeted / deliberate)
- Violence against animals (excessive / abuse / cruelty)
- Suicide / self-harm
- Emotional abuse (global)
- Physical abuse (against PCs)
- Sexual violence (global)
# Veils
- Cannibalism - *Offscreen & minimum detail*
- Anti-Civilian Atrocities (Individual / Boots-On-Ground Scale) - *Can happen offscreen and see news reports; don't put PCs inside scene as it unfolds*
- Violence against children (untargeted / collateral damage)
- Torture (global) - *Minimum detail*
- Sex (global; no subcategory restrictions) - *Fade to black / minimum detail*
- Sex work - *Minimum detail*
- Physical abuse (against NPCs) - *Offscreen*
# Caution
- Institutional / military / police brutality - *Avoid obvious real-world resonance*
- Bigotry / discrimination / dehumanization (in-universe groups only) - *Be aware of obvious real-world resonance; by NPCs only*
- Space / hard vacuum exposure - *heads up*
- Grief & mourning - *heads up, possible step back*
- Human experimentation - *limit detail and stick to sci-fi for distancing, not the more fucked-up real-world examples*
- Child soldiers - *retain targeted violence line; backstory only if at all*
- Other harm to children - *pull back detail, don't dwell*
- Disagreement / heated argument / conflict escalation / catastrophic relationship blowup between PCs *- Check in above table, make sure everyone's on the same page*
- Romance (general) - *as with conflict, check in above table*
