# Character Creation
1. [[Rules/2 Character Creation#Mech Aspects\|Mech Gear]]: You do *not* have a backup weapon of any kind. Your two gear aspects are it. See my explanation on *Gundam 00*'s initial team composition in the discord for why.

# NPCs
Additions to the [[Rules/5 Running The Game#Running NPCs\|5 Running The Game#Running NPCs]] rules:

All NPCs have a Faction property, even if it points to [[Database/Factions/Independent\|Independent]]. 

## Aspects
In addition to the Relationship aspect, all minor or major NPCs have a second aspect slot reserved for their **LOYALTY** to their faction. This is how they feel about the organization they're part of and how they understand themselves within it. Are they a loyal attack dog? A reformer? A traitor? This can stay blank until defined; it cannot be used as a free space for something else.

If an NPC is a member of multiple factions, this aspect can be about one of them, some of them, or all of them, as best fits the fiction.

So, when reading an NPC sheet, their first three aspects are always:
1. High Concept
2. Relationship (to the PCs)
3. Loyalty (to their faction)
4. Free aspects

# Factions
We are using *some of* the [[Rules/Fate SRD/Factions (UNEDITED SRD)\|Factions (UNEDITED SRD)]] rules. I have not yet thought about how to implement changing faction beliefs, except that fate points cannot be used in the attempt by either side - there must be a free invoke available on any aspect being brought into the roll. 

## Scale & Influence
Factions have a [[Rules/2 Character Creation#Scale\|2 Character Creation#Scale]], like mechs, and use the same rules when interacting with each other. Faction scale ranges from 1 to 5. Unlike physical scale, faction scale represents the scope and reach of the faction, and its capacity to influence other factions. 

Characters have Influence ranks with their factions that range from 0 to 3. Any character attempting an opposed roll against a faction adds their Influence as a flat bonus to their result, while the faction adds its Scale to its side.
