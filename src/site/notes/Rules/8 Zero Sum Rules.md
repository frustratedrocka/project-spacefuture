# Character Creation
1. [[Rules/2 Character Creation#Mech Aspects\|Mech Gear]]: You do *not* have a backup weapon of any kind. Your two gear aspects are it. See my explanation on *Gundam 00*'s initial team composition in the discord for why.
2. The [[Rules/2 Character Creation#Free Space\|2 Character Creation#Free Space]] aspect is reserved for a **CONNECTION** to something outside yourself.

# NPCs
Additions to the [[Rules/5 Running The Game#Running NPCs\|5 Running The Game#Running NPCs]] rules:

All NPCs have a Faction property, even if it points to [[Database/Factions/Independent\|Independent]]. 

## Aspects
In addition to the Relationship aspect, all minor or major NPCs have a second aspect slot reserved for their **LOYALTY** to their faction. This is how they feel about the organization they're part of and how they understand themselves within it. Are they a loyal attack dog? A reformer? A traitor? This can stay blank until defined; it cannot be used as a free space for something else.

So, when reading an NPC sheet, their first three aspects are always:
1. High Concept
2. Relationship (to the PCs)
3. Loyalty (to their faction)
4. Free aspects
