A standard human-scale space suit, for when you need protection from hard vacuum but don't feel like getting into [[Database/Glossary/Mobile Suit\|a 20-meter-tall engine of destruction]].

Normal suits always include life support and communications equipment and are typically fitted with a [[Database/Glossary/Datapad\|Datapad]] mount / dock on the inner forearm, as well as a [[Database/Glossary/Lift Grip\|Lift Grip]], mag grapple, mag boots and switch, and the rest of the standard [[Database/Glossary/Ship#Getting around\|shipboard belt package]]. 

Optionally, a normal suit can be fitted with a human-scale thruster pack. These are primarily used by hangar crews or ship technicians during EVA. 

Pilot suits are lightweight variants that sacrifice some thermal regulation, air capacity, and battery life to maximize mobility. This is the version you wear most of the time, but you do have a standard version available if needed. The standard mobile suit [[Database/Glossary/Mobile Suit#Safety First?\|life pod]] more than compensates for these tradeoffs, unless its own life support systems are damaged.
