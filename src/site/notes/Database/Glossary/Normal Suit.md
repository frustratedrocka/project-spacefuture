A standard human-scale space suit, for when you need protection from hard vacuum but don't feel like getting into [[Database/Glossary/Mobile Suit\|a 20-meter-tall engine of destruction]].

Normal suits always include life support and communications equipment and are typically equipped with a [[Database/Glossary/Datapad\|Datapad]], as well as a [[Database/Glossary/Lift Grip\|Lift Grip]], mag grapple, mag boots and switch, and the rest of the standard [[Database/Glossary/Ship\|shipboard]] belt package. 

Optionally, a normal suit can be fitted with a human-scale thruster pack. These are primarily used by hangar crews or ship technicians during EVA. 

Pilot suits are lightweight variants that sacrifice insulation, air capacity, and battery life to maximize mobility. This is the version you wear most of the time, but you do have a standard version available if needed.
