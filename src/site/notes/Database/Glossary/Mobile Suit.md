Why are mobile suits even a thing:

In a word, versatility. They tie (and occasionally win) most tradeoffs vs anything else in their roles, and they fill *most* major combat armor roles. They've got less raw speed than a strike fighter or bomber, but are *much* more agile, their firing arcs aren't  restricted by thrust vectors, and are easier to store more of in less hangar space. They're much bigger and less armor-efficient than tanks, but pack much more powerful weaponry and are effectively all-terrain. And, most importantly, the same mobile suit can usually handle space and groundbound operations just fine, even if they're often designed with one or the other in mind.

The biggest notable exceptions are transports and terrestrial aircraft. "Box with engines" is still unchallenged for getting lots of people and gear from point A to point B, and mobile suits don't have the thrust-to-weight ratio (and certainly not the aerodynamics) needed to fly in atmosphere and gravity, even weaker Martian gravity.

Unless someone wants to have a transforming suit like the Zeta, ZZ, Delta, Asshimar, Xi, ReZel, etc. in which case that technology can have gotten out of the r&d idea phase and into prototyping already.
