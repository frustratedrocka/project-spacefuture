Getting to and from [[Database/Places/Jupiter Sphere/Jupiter\|Jupiter]] is not a fast process. It's hardly impossible, but it's a commitment, especially depending on where you happen to be relative to its 12-year solar orbit. 

A sufficiently powerful ship can make the trip on its own; many do. For everyone else, there's the Charun-class ferries. These gargantuan transports never actually stop, they just slow down to let smaller ships dock and undock in / near [[Database/Places/The Belt\|the Belt]], then redirect their momentum back out towards Jupiter. Jovebound ships undock close enough to maneuver and slow down on their own, beltbound ships dock on the second half of the approach, then the ferry swings around the dark side of the gas giant, is refuelled in motion by a dedicated platform, and slingshots back towards the sun.
